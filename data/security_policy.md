# Nimbus Coffee Co. - IT Security Policy

This is a made-up company handbook used to test DocChat.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager, Vaultkeep, to generate and store them. Never reuse a work password on another site.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required on email, the HR portal and Ledgerly. Use an authenticator app; text-message codes are not allowed.

## Lost devices

If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT help desk within 1 hour by calling extension 4357 (HELP). IT will remotely wipe the device.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to phish@nimbus.example. Do not click links or open attachments in them. Every employee completes phishing training twice a year.
