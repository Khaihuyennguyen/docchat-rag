# Nimbus Coffee Co. - Leave Policy

This is a made-up company handbook used to test DocChat.

## Vacation

Full-time employees earn 18 vacation days per calendar year. Vacation days accrue monthly at 1.5 days per month. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year; the rest expire on December 31.

Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance. Requests during the holiday rush (November 20 to January 5) need approval from a regional manager.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over. If you are sick for more than 3 days in a row, please provide a doctor's note to HR.

## Parental leave

Nimbus offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, including adoptive and foster parents. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the child's arrival.

## Bereavement

You may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member, and 2 days for extended family.
