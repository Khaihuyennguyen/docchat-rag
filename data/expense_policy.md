# Nimbus Coffee Co. - Expense Policy

This is a made-up company handbook used to test DocChat.

## Meals while travelling

The daily meal allowance on business trips is $65 per day within the country and $90 per day abroad. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Hotels and flights

Book flights in economy class through the Skyline travel tool. Flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy. Hotels must cost no more than $220 per night unless a manager approves the exception in writing.

## Home office

Each employee may claim a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts are required.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense in the Ledgerly app within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. Expenses over $500 need sign-off from your manager and from finance. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run.
